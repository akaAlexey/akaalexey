# Иллюстрации сайтов AGL и akaalexey

Использован встроенный image_gen; CLI и платный API отдельно не применялись. Финальные изображения сохранены в dist/assets/ как WebP; JPEG используются для обложек ссылок. Выполнена техническая оптимизация размеров и формата без изменения художественного содержания.

| Файл | Назначение |
| --- | --- |
| agl-hero.webp | Главный экран AGL |
| agl-mobile.webp | Мобильная композиция AGL |
| agl-studio.webp | Иллюстрация работы над игрой |
| alexey-portrait.webp | Аниме-портрет Алексея |
| alexey-workspace.webp | Иллюстрация личного сайта |
| rublemet-cover.webp | Промо-иллюстрация «Рублемёта» |

Аниме-образ используется с разрешения владельца по предоставленному референсу. Изображения игрового мира rublemet-street.webp и rublemet-character.webp взяты из материалов AGL, просмотрены перед использованием и подписаны как иллюстрации. Они не выдаются за снимки интерфейса. На AGL отсутствуют другие персональные профили.

## Промпты

### agl-hero

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: wide website hero illustration for Alex Game Lab, an independent game team. Create a refined tactile 3D miniature of an imaginative game-development workbench: a small modular city block, tiny faceless geometric game pieces, a few storyboarding cards without writing, a floating translucent violet frame, a restrained lime light. Single cohesive composition, elegant rather than toy clutter. The miniature is on a dark charcoal circular plinth toward the right half of the image; ample near-black negative space on the left. Premium matte clay, brushed dark metal, frosted glass. Dark graphite #0B0C16 background, soft lavender illumination and selective pale lime accents. Wide landscape 16:9. Beautiful photographic rendering of a clearly fictional miniature, crisp details, soft shadows. No people, no letters, no logos, no numbers, no watermark, no real money, no legible screens. This is a website illustration, not a depiction of an actual office.

Прозрачный фон: нет.

### alexey-portrait

Use case: identity-preserve. Asset type: personal website avatar. Use the attached image as the exact character identity reference. Draw the SAME young adult anime man, keeping face shape, warm brown eyes, short tousled dark-brown hair, rectangular dark glasses, calm confident slight smile, blue subtly patterned button-up shirt. Refined clean hand-drawn anime illustration with restrained realistic shading matching the original. A beautifully composed head-and-upper-torso portrait, subject facing the viewer, shoulders fully included, centered. Genuine transparent background, clean alpha edges, no circle or frame, no props, no text, no logo, no watermark. Do not change identity, age, glasses, hair color or shirt color. New portrait framing of the supplied anime persona, not a photograph.

Прозрачный фон: да.

No words, no logos, no numbers, no watermark, no additional people.

### rublemet-cover

Use case: compositing. Asset type: promotional illustration for the Android story clicker Rublemet, to be labeled as artwork rather than a gameplay screenshot. Image 1 is the game's canonical street environment. Image 2 is its canonical character. Make a wide landscape 16:9 promotional composition preserving these two designs. Same warm golden-hour illustrated street with kiosk at LEFT, open cobblestone foreground, workshop on RIGHT, distant town and orange sky. Place the same friendly stocky mustached man from Image 2 naturally on the RIGHT foreground, in the exact cream suit, patterned gold waistcoat, dark shirt and brown shoes, keeping his face and character proportions. His body is realistically grounded in the street perspective and he gives the same relaxed confident gesture. Keep the LEFT half relatively spacious for website text overlay outside the bitmap. Fine stylized game illustration, amber light, detailed believable architecture. No additional characters, no words, no cash, no UI, no store badges, no logos, no watermark. Do not change the character's clothing or the kiosk's visual identity.

Прозрачный фон: нет.
